Everyone says 20% of customers make 80% of sales. Is it true for your business? Was it more
or less true last year? And does every region behave the same way? The native way to find out
is a sorted column chart, a cumulative DAX measure and a dual axis — and it still cannot give
you the answer in words.

This walkthrough answers all three questions on 5,000 customers with Pareto Chart Pro: two
fields, no DAX. A setup guide inside the visual walks you through it, and lets you try every
Pro feature on your own data with one click.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE
• The setup guide: your fields, then one-click "Try" buttons for the Pro features on your own
  data, then the style templates
• The answer in one sentence: "1,120 of 5,000 customers (22.4%) make 80% of sales", counted
  customer by customer
• Comparison with last year: comparison bars, a dashed cumulative line and the change on
  every bar in percentage points (and why points, not percent)
• Small multiples: one Pareto per region on a shared scale — and the one region that behaves
  differently
• ABC classification, cut at the exact customer where the curve crosses 80% and 95%
• Drilldown from segment to customer, with named bars when there are 30 or fewer
• Highlighting vs filtering from other visuals, and how to switch with Edit interactions
• Style templates — Clean, Minimal, Contrast, Soft, Compact and an IBCS style — and a legend
• Format on the canvas: click the bars, the line or an axis to format them
• Keyboard navigation, and the visual in 12 languages

FREE AND PRO
The free version is a complete Pareto chart with the summary sentence, named bars,
conditional formatting, threshold colours, drilldown and cross-filtering. Style templates, the
legend, the setup guide, format on the canvas and 12 languages are free too. Pro adds period
comparison, small multiples, ABC zones, bin sizes from 1% to 20%, outlier exclusion and value
labels — and the setup guide lets you try each one on your own data before buying, under a
"Pro preview" watermark while you edit.

PRIVACY
No network requests, no telemetry: everything is calculated inside Power BI. The links on
the start screen open only when you click them.
