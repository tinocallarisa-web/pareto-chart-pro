1. License tiers

Free tier Free

Available to all Power BI users at no charge. Includes:

  • Full Pareto chart with ranked bars and cumulative % line
  • 5 bins displayed (fixed at 20% bin size)
  • Up to 2 configurable reference lines (default: 80%)
  • Drilldown and hierarchical view
  • Cross-filtering and multi-select
  • Conditional formatting on bar color (the fx rule dialog)
  • Threshold Colors — colour bins by cumulative % threshold
  • Tooltips field well — up to 10 additional measures
  • IBCS Mode — one-click International Business Communication Standards styling
  • Keyboard navigation and screen-reader support
  • High contrast mode
  • Bar color and axis styling

Pro tier Pro

Requires an active AppSource subscription. Includes all Free features plus:

  • Custom bin size: 1–20% (up to 100 bars)
  • Outlier exclusion — filter top and bottom % of entities
  • Value labels on bars (font size, color, % sign)
  • Third reference line
  • Bar border color and width
  • Bar gap control

A 30-day free trial is available on AppSource. No credit card required to start the trial.
