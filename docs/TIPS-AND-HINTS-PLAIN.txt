1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
- Entity: Drag a categorical field or hierarchy (Customer, Product, SKU) into Entity.
- Value: Drag your primary numeric measure (Sales, Liters, Defect Count) into Value.
- Tooltips: Drag up to 10 additional measures (Profit, Margin %, Quantity) into Tooltips.

2. BIG DATA ARCHITECTURE (< 30K WITHOUT DAX vs > 30K WITH DAX)
--------------------------------------------------------------
A. Datasets < 30,000 Entities (WITHOUT DAX):
   - Simply drag raw IDs (e.g. customer_id, SKU) directly into Entity.
   - No DAX required. Pareto Chart Pro automatically bins, ranks, and calculates cumulative percentages natively.

B. Datasets > 30,000 up to 150,000+ Entities (WITH DAX):
   - Create a DAX calculated column to group entities into quantile bins and place it in Entity.
   - This delivers instant (<1ms) cross-filtering of all 150,000+ rows across your entire report.

   EXACT DAX CALCULATED COLUMN FORMULA EXAMPLE:
   --------------------------------------------
   Grupo Pareto = 
   VAR VentasActual = 'sales_data'[Sales]
   VAR TotalFilas = COUNTROWS(ALL('sales_data'))
   VAR Ranking = RANKX(ALL('sales_data'), 'sales_data'[Sales], VentasActual, DESC, Skip)
   VAR Percentil = CEILING((Ranking / TotalFilas) * 10, 1)
   RETURN "Grupo " & FORMAT(Percentil, "00")

   (Note: Change * 10 to * 50 if you prefer 50 fine-grained quantile groups).

3. FORMAT PANE HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------------
- IBCS Mode (Free & Pro): Toggle under Pareto card for International Business Communication Standards corporate neutral styling (#404040 charcoal bars, #000000 solid axis typography).
- Bin Size % (Pro): Adjust bin size from 1% to 20% (up to 100 bars). Free tier is fixed at 20% (5 bars).
- Outlier Trimming (Pro): Exclude top or bottom % outliers to keep Pareto bins clean.
- Value Labels (Pro): Display percentages directly above bars with customizable font size and color.
- Reference Lines: Up to 3 configurable crosshairs (default 80% threshold).
