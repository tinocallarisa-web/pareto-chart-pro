PARETO CHART PRO (v1.2.0.0) — QUICK USER GUIDE & TIPS
===================================================================

1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
- Entity: Drag a categorical field or hierarchy (Customer, Product, SKU) into Entity.
- Value: Drag your primary numeric measure (Sales, Liters, Defect Count) into Value.
- Tooltips: Drag up to 10 additional measures (Profit, Margin %, Quantity) into Tooltips.

2. BIG DATA SCALING (150,000+ ROWS)
-----------------------------------
- Massive Datasets (>30k rows): Use a DAX calculated column (e.g. [Grupo Pareto] with 10 or 50 quantile groups) in Entity. This processes 150,000+ rows instantly with <1ms cross-filtering across your entire report.
- Standard Datasets (<30k rows): Place raw IDs (e.g. customer_id) directly in Entity. Pareto Chart Pro automatically bins, ranks, and calculates cumulative percentages.

3. FORMAT PANE HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------------
- IBCS Mode (Pro): Toggle under Pareto card for International Business Communication Standards corporate neutral styling (#404040 charcoal bars, #000000 solid axis typography).
- Bin Size % (Pro): Adjust bin size from 1% to 20% (up to 100 bars). Free tier is fixed at 20% (5 bars).
- Outlier Trimming (Pro): Exclude top or bottom % outliers to keep Pareto bins clean.
- Value Labels (Pro): Display percentages directly above bars with customizable font size and color.
- Reference Lines: Up to 3 configurable crosshairs (default 80% threshold).

4. FREE VS PRO FEATURE COMPARISON
----------------------------------
- Pareto Bars & Cumulative Line ..... Free: YES ..... Pro: YES
- Max Rows Supported ................ Free: 150,000+ . Pro: 150,000+
- Bin Count ......................... Free: 5 (20%) . Pro: 1–100 (1–20%)
- Custom Tooltips Measures .......... Free: Basic ... Pro: Up to 10 Extra Measures
- IBCS Mode (Corporate Palette) ..... Free: NO ...... Pro: YES
- Value Labels on Bars .............. Free: NO ...... Pro: YES
- Outlier Trimming .................. Free: NO ...... Pro: YES
- Reference Lines ................... Free: Up to 2 . Pro: Up to 3
